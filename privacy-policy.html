Privacy Policy for Team Timezones Extension

Last Updated:

Introduction

Team Timezones ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension ("Extension") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

The Team Timezones extension collects minimal information necessary to provide its functionality:

  • Home Base Preferences: Your selected home base city name and timezone
  • Team Member Information: City names and timezones for team members you add
  • User Preferences: Any custom time selections you make within the extension

Important: We do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, address, etc.)
  • Browsing history or website data
  • Location data beyond timezone preferences
  • Any information from websites you visit
  • Analytics or tracking data

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To display current and future times for your selected cities
  • To calculate time differences between your home base and team member locations
  • To remember your preferences between browser sessions
  • To provide the core timezone comparison functionality

Data Storage

All data collected by this extension is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API (chrome.storage.sync). This means:

  • Your data is stored on your computer or device
  • Data may be synchronized across your Chrome browsers if you're signed into Chrome
  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • No data is stored on our servers

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT share, sell, rent, or disclose your information to any third parties. Specifically:

  • We do not transmit data to external servers
  • We do not share data with advertisers
  • We do not share data with analytics services
  • We do not sell or monetize your data

Your data remains private and is only used within the extension on your device.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's secure storage API, your information is protected by:

  • Chrome's built-in security measures
  • Your device's security settings
  • The fact that no data leaves your device

User Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: You can view all stored data through the extension's settings page
  • Modification: You can add, edit, or remove team members and home base settings at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete all extension data by uninstalling the extension or clearing Chrome's extension storage

To delete all data: Uninstall the extension from Chrome, or use Chrome's developer tools to clear extension storage.

Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, APIs, or external resources. All functionality is provided by:

  • Built-in JavaScript APIs (Intl.DateTimeFormat for timezone calculations)
  • Chrome extension APIs (chrome.storage for data persistence)

No external network requests are made, and no third-party code is loaded.

Children's Privacy

Our extension does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect personal information, this extension is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
